A newspaper-style front page summing up Sunday 22 November 2026's world news, drawn from scratch with PIL.

Lead story: **COP31 collapsed** — the climate summit in Antalya ended with no deal after the EU rejected a text with no mention of fossil fuels.

Also covered: Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain; 'El Chapo Isidro' captured in Sinaloa; a Russian missile hits Kryvyi Rih; US stocks slide into correction; the World Court rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya; the EU and Indonesia sign a landmark trade deal.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (standard library only) calls `draw_image.py`, which draws the 1024x1024 PNG deterministically with PIL and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `out/`). Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
